## Reproduced findings

- pnpm 12 did not apply the older `package.json` policy location. Move policies
  into `pnpm-workspace.yaml`, retain the build-script allowlist and override the
  affected transitive tooling dependencies. The dependency audit went from 13
  advisories (six high, four moderate, three low) to zero; peer validation also
  passed. The Docker dependency stage must copy the workspace policy file.
- Initial animation-loader and documentation-TOC geometry reads could force
  layout, failing the existing Lighthouse assertion after the tooling update.
  Read initial geometry after layout with ResizeObserver; preserve deferred
  animation loading, reduced motion, restored-scroll settling and TOC updates.

The original Lighthouse engine and every assertion/size budget remain in place.
A trial upgrade that removed audit IDs was rejected rather than disabling those
assertions. No dependency was added to the product runtime for this audit.
